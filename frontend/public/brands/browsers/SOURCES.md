# Browser and app icons

`vivo.svg`, `oppo.svg`, `dingtalk.svg`, `douyin.svg`, `yandex.svg` and `quark.svg` were supplied by the instance owner
on 2026-11-06 for the browser report. Only the XML declaration, DOCTYPE and editor attributes (`t`, `class`, `p-id`,
`width`, `height`) were removed; the shapes and colours are unchanged. They identify the browsers and apps visitors use;
each mark belongs to its owner and no rights are granted. They were checked for scripts, event handlers, external
references and embedded images.
